# Trailbound Soulmates: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Trailbound Soulmates ("the app") is a game by Wise Owl Tech, LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data.

## What we collect
**We do not collect, store or receive any personal data.** There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics of our own and no servers operated by us that receive your gameplay. Your progress — your journey, journal, names and settings — is saved only on your device and never leaves it except as described below. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app is free and shows ads using Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and — only with your permission — personalise ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use data from your device, including:

- **Identifiers:** your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and other device identifiers
- **Device and connection information:** device model, operating system, IP address and network information
- **Usage and interaction data:** app launches, ad impressions, taps and other ad interactions
- **Diagnostics:** crash, performance and ad-delivery diagnostics

See [How Google uses data from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

- A short interstitial ad may appear occasionally at a natural break in play (when a new in-game day begins after the nightly Soulmate Sync question), and you can optionally watch a rewarded ad to double a bonus. Ads never appear during gameplay events or while two phones are paired.
- **Consent:** where required (for example the EEA and UK), the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before personalised ads load, and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks your permission before the advertising identifier is used for tracking. Declining is fine — the game plays exactly the same, with non-personalised ads.
- **Controls:** you can change or withdraw tracking permission any time in iOS *Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking*, and limit ad personalisation in *Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising*.

## Game features and your data
- **On-device AI:** the game's narration and generated events are produced by an AI model that runs entirely on your device. Nothing you type or play is sent anywhere for this. Downloading the optional model file is a one-way download; it sends no gameplay data.
- **Your own server (optional):** if you choose, in settings, to point the Game Master at a remote Ollama server, the narration prompts are sent to that server — it is a server you control or choose, not ours, and this is off unless you configure it.
- **Soulmate Bump and Sync:** pairing with a second iPhone happens directly between the two devices over your local network and Bluetooth using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity and Nearby Interaction. It involves no server and no data leaves the two phones.
- **File sharing:** the app lets you copy an AI model file into the app using Finder or the Files app. That is you moving your own file; nothing is uploaded.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Ad requests are not tagged for child-directed treatment, and the app carries ads as described above.

## Data sharing and security
We do not sell data — we hold none to sell. Data processed by Google for advertising is governed by Google's policies linked above. Network traffic described here uses encrypted connections.
